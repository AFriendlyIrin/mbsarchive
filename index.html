Minutes Before Sunset

Script and text archive for Minutes Before Sunset, a romhack of Fire Emblem: The Sacred Stones by Rivian. It is a distant sequel to Cerulean Crescent.

Fulfil the prophecy as it was foretold

Ending Cards

Village Elder
Village Elder returned home a celebrated legend. He never grew weary of the fame and made sure to retell those five minutes of glory in detail. He regretted his vanity when his tales inspired his grandkids to abandon home in chase of glory.

Child Attendant (girl)
Child Attendant 2 was content to coast off her past feats, but the growing hunger forced her from home. After driving a few species of dragon to extinction, she abused her power to form a religion that could keep her well fed.

Child Attendant (boy)
Child Attendant 1 was determined to not let his finest hour stay at age seven. He attempted increasingly dangerous feats, the most recent being global conquest. Any rumours that he listened to demonic whispers were eradicated.

Elusive Prophet
With the prophecy fulfilled, the Prophet returned to her home in the mountains. She made sure all her future prophecies would be annoyingly vague to prevent any possibility of them being wrong.
